# Finance

**Menu:** Financial Analysis, Financial Reporting, Treasury Operations,
Capital Management, Tax Planning. Managers also see a **Finance Manager**
menu with Financial Planning, Budget & Forecasting, Treasury Management,
Investment Management, and Financial Reports.

**Who can access it:** Finance staff — and since Finance and Accounting share
the same backend pages, Accounting staff can use these too. See the
[Accounting guide](01-accounting.md) for the underlying Budget, Audit, Bank
Reconciliation, and Tax pages.

## Dashboard (`/fin/`)

Cash position, budget summary, audit summary, and tax summary tiles.

## Cash Flow

- **Cash Flow Statement** (`/gl/cash-flow/`) — read-only, date-range
  filterable.
- **13-Week Cash Forecast** (`/fin/forecast/`) — rolling forward-looking cash
  projection.

## Budgets, Audits, Bank Reconciliation, Tax Filings

These are the same pages documented in the Accounting guide, reached from the
Finance menu at `/fin/budgets/`, `/fin/audits/`, `/fin/bank-rec/`, and
`/fin/tax/`:

- **Budgets** — create/edit budget lines, view Budget-vs-Actual.
- **Audits** — schedule and complete internal audits with findings
  (severity, department, description).
- **Bank Reconciliation** — reconcile bank statements against your books.
- **Tax Filings** — track filing type, jurisdiction, period, amounts, and
  status.
