# Marketing

**Menu:** Campaigns, Market Research, Advertising, Analytics, Content
Management, Social Media, Email Marketing. Managers also see a **Marketing
Manager** menu with Marketing Budget, Campaign Approvals, and Marketing
Reports.

**Who can access it:** Marketing staff only.

## Dashboard (`/mkt/`)

Campaign status/channel charts, budget-by-channel, lead status/source,
content status.

## Campaigns (`/mkt/campaigns/`)

Create or edit a campaign: name, channel, objective, owner, start/end date,
budget, status, notes.

## Leads (`/mkt/leads/`)

Create or edit a lead: name, company, email, source, owner, captured date,
status.

## Content (`/mkt/content/`)

Create or edit a content item: title, type, channel, author, due/publish
date, status.

## Ads (`/mkt/ads/`)

Create or edit an ad: name, channel, campaign, budget, spend, impressions,
clicks, conversions, dates, status, owner.

## Market Research (`/mkt/research/`)

Create or edit a research project: title, type, description, owner, dates,
budget, findings.

## Analytics (`/mkt/analytics/`)

Website, campaign, and sales analytics dashboard.

## Marketing Budget (`/mkt/budget/`)

Manager-only menu leaf. Create or edit a budget request line: item,
campaign, category, amount, requested by, request date, status, notes.
