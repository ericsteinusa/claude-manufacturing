# Time Clock

**Menu:** reached from the **Personnel** menu ("Time Clock") rather than its
own top-level department — Clock In/Out, View Hours, Time Off Requests,
Schedules, Overtime Reports, Attendance Reports, Shift Management.

**Who can access it:** Clocking in/out and viewing your own hours is open to
every employee. Attendance, device management, and "everyone's" overtime
report are HR/manager-only.

## Clock In/Out (`/time-clock/`)

Shows your current punch status and today's entries, with **Clock In** /
**Clock Out** buttons.

## View Hours (`/time-clock/hours/`)

Today/week/month totals for you, the logged-in employee.

## Attendance (`/time-clock/attendance/`)

HR/managers only — an all-employee present/absent view for a chosen date.

## Time Clock Devices (`/time-clock/devices/`)

HR/Personnel-role only. Register a physical clock terminal (name, location,
type, IP/port, config), enable/disable it, trigger an immediate poll, delete
it, and view its sync log.

## Overtime Report (`/time-clock/ot/`)

Toggle between "mine" (any employee, their own overtime) and "all"
(managers/full-access — every employee's overtime), with a date-range
filter.

## Schedule (`/time-clock/schedule/`)

A shift/schedule summary derived from clock-in records, date-range
filterable.

## Time Off Requests

Same pages as documented in the [Personnel guide](11-personnel.md#time-off-requests-time-off) — submit your own request; HR/managers approve or deny.
