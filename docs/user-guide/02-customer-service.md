# Customer Service

**Menu:** Customer Service Menu (Customer Entry, Open Tickets, Customer
Accounts, Returns & Refunds, Knowledge Base, Service Reports, Surveys &
Feedback). Managers also see a **CS Manager** menu with Ticket Reports, Staff
Management, Customer Satisfaction, and Escalations.

**Who can access it:** Customer Service, Sales, and Customers staff share
these ticket screens.

## Dashboard (`/cs-dash/`)

Ticket volume trend, return status/reason breakdown, escalation counts.

## Tickets (`/cs/`)

- **List** — search, filter by status, toggle "My Tickets," see open/
  high-priority counts.
- **New ticket** (`/cs/new/`) — pick the customer, enter call notes, date/
  time, and comments.
- **Detail** (`/cs/<id>/`) — edit call notes/comments/completion date, and
  **Close Ticket** when resolved.

## Escalations (`/cs/escalations/`)

Shows every open high- or critical-priority ticket, for follow-up.

## Reports (`/cs/reports/`)

Daily/weekly summary, resolution/SLA/CSAT reports, with a date-range
selector.

## Improvement Plans (`/cs/plans/`)

Create or update an improvement plan: title, description, owner, target
date, status.

## Returns & Refunds (`/cs/returns/`)

Create a return (customer, date, reason, items returned, refund amount) and
update its status as it's processed.

## Knowledge Base (`/cs/kb/`)

Create or edit an article: title, category, content, author, publish date,
status, tags.

## Surveys & Feedback (`/cs/surveys/`)

Create a survey (title, description, type, dates), record a customer
response (score, comments), and view aggregated results.
