# Personnel (HR)

**Menu:** Personnel CRM, Registration Form, Update Password, Display
Department, Dept Entry, Dept Sub Entry, Time Clock, Employee Records,
Benefits, Performance Reviews, Disciplinary Records, Training & Development,
Onboarding. Managers also see a **Personnel Manager** menu with Hiring &
Recruitment, Terminations, Salary Management, and HR Reports.

**Who can access it:** Personnel staff, and anyone whose role is **HR /
Personnel** — that role grants access regardless of which department the
person is actually filed under.

## Dashboard (`/pers/`)

Headcount by department, hire trend, time-off status, training status,
review ratings, payroll-by-month.

## Employee Records (`/people/`)

Full employee record: name, employee ID, email, job title, address,
department/sub-department. List, create, edit, and export to CSV.

## Departments & Sub-Departments (`/pers/depts/`)

Create or rename a department or sub-department.

## Time Off Requests (`/time-off/`)

Any employee submits their own request (start/end date, type — Vacation,
Sick, etc. — notes). HR and managers approve or deny pending requests.

## Performance Reviews (`/pers/reviews/`)

Schedule a review: employee, type, date, reviewer, rating, status, notes.

## Training & Development (`/pers/training/`)

Schedule a course: employee, course name, type, start/end date, status.

## Skills Matrix (`/skills/`)

Define required skills per role, record each employee's skill level, and
view a competency-gap summary (overall and per-employee).

## Benefits (`/benefits/`)

Define benefit plans and enroll/view an employee's benefit elections.

## Recruiting / ATS (`/ats/`)

Create job requisitions, track candidates, and manage applications tied to a
requisition — the full hiring pipeline.

## Terminations (`/pers/terminations/`)

Only linked from the Personnel Manager menu (the page itself has no
additional role restriction). Initiate a termination record for an employee
(termination date, type, reason, status, rehire eligibility, notes); each
record has its own detail page (`/pers/terminations/<id>/`) for tracking
status.

## Exit Interviews (`/pers/exit-interviews/`)

Log an exit interview: employee, interview date, interviewer, reason for
leaving, feedback, whether they'd recommend the company, notes.

## Offboarding (`/pers/offboarding/`)

Track offboarding checklist tasks per departing employee (task, status, due
date, assigned to, notes) and mark tasks complete.

## Workforce Analytics (`/workforce/`)

Create a headcount plan and track per-employee employment dates.

## Employee Self-Service (`/ess/`)

Every logged-in employee, in any department, can use this to view their own
pay stubs and YTD summary, submit/view their own time-off, view their own
performance reviews and training records, and edit their own profile.
